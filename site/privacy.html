Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

GTA 6 Clipped ("we") runs a private publishing tool that posts videos made by us to social media accounts that we own. This policy explains what the tool does with data.

Who uses the tool

Only the owner of the GTA 6 Clipped accounts. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

What we access

When we connect our own TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, the platform gives the tool an access token and basic profile information (account name and ID). The tool uses these only to upload our own videos to our own account and to check whether an upload finished.

What we store

Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on our private server, readable only by the tool. We store the links and IDs of the videos we publish. We do not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people, and we do not read other users' content, followers, messages or analytics.

Sharing

We do not share data with anyone. Videos are sent only to the platform we are posting to.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the app in the platform's settings revokes access. We delete stored tokens as soon as an account is disconnected or on request.
