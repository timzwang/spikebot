GTA 6 Clipped

GTA 6 Clipped is a short-form video channel. We post the best moments from Grand Theft Auto VI streams as captioned clips, plus edits made from Rockstar Games' official trailers, on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels.

How the channel works

  • Clips come only from streamers who allow clipping through their official clipping programmes.
  • Every video adds something of our own: captions, a hook and context. We never re-upload streams unchanged.
  • Our software, Shorts Uploader, finds moments, prepares the videos and sends them to the channel owner for review. Nothing is posted until the channel owner approves it.

Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of GTA 6 Clipped to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users. It uses:

  • the YouTube Data API to upload approved Shorts to our own channel, set their thumbnails and read our channel's view and subscriber totals;
  • the TikTok Content Posting API to send approved videos to our own TikTok account;
  • the Instagram API to publish approved Reels to our own Instagram account.
