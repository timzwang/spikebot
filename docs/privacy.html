Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers GTA 6 Clipped and Shorts Uploader, the private software the channel owner uses to publish the channel's own videos. Shorts Uploader has one user: the channel owner. It does not let anyone else sign in.

Accounts we connect

Shorts Uploader connects only to the channel owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube (Google): to upload approved videos to our channel, set thumbnails, and read our channel's own statistics (views, subscribers, video count). Scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • TikTok: to read our account's basic profile and posting options, and to post or send approved videos to our own account.
  • Instagram: to publish approved Reels to our own account and read their view counts.

We do not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube, TikTok or Instagram user. We do not read other people's videos, comments, subscriptions or private information through these services.

What we store

  • Access and refresh tokens for our own accounts, stored only on our private server, never shared and never published.
  • Links, titles and view totals of the videos we have posted, so we can track what has gone out.
  • To find highlight moments, the software counts messages in public Twitch and Kick stream chats. It keeps a few sample message texts per moment for review, without usernames, and does not contact or profile chat users.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use any of it for advertising. Videos are sent only to the platform they are being published on.

Retention and deletion

Working video files are deleted within a few days of being posted, rejected or expiring. Tokens are kept only while the connection is in use and are deleted when it is removed. Our use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access to a Google account can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. TikTok and Instagram access can be removed in each app's settings under connected or authorised apps.

YouTube and Google

Shorts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.
